Sourya Roy

I am an assistant professor in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Iowa. Before this, I was a data scientist at Foursquare. I did my Ph.D. in Computer Science from UC Riverside, where I was fortunate to be advised by Silas Richelson and Amey Bhangale. My primary research interest lies in theoretical computer science, specifically in exploring pseudorandomness and its connections and applications to coding theory, cryptography, and related areas. I am also interested in computer vision and have worked on several applied ML/computer vision projects.

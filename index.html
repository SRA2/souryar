Hello, I’m Sourya

I am an assistant professor at the University of Iowa - Computer Science department. Before this, I was a data scientist at Foursquare. I did my PhD in Computer Science from UC Riverside, where I was fortuante to be advised by Silas Richelson and Amey Bhangale. My primary research interest lies in theoretical computer science, specifically in exploring pseudorandomness and its connections and applications to areas such as coding theory, cryptography, etc. I am also interested in computer vision and worked on several applied ML/computer vision projects.
